Membership

Membership Packages

All memberships grant full access to the ranges, club equipment, and online booking portal. Equipment hire is included in all target bookings and classes.

Target Booking

For Members

Target booking is free for all annual and monthly members. Members can reserve a shooting lane up to 7 days in advance through our online portal. Choose from 18-metre indoor lanes or outdoor distances.

For Guests

Guest archers pay £15 per 2-hour session. All guests must hold a valid Archery GB card. Equipment hire is included in the booking.

Training Courses

6-Week Beginner Induction

For adults new to archery. Covers safety, technique, and equipment. Next intake: 13 October.

Junior Archery Academy

Structured programme for ages 8–16. Young archers learn fundamental skills and progress through achievement levels.

Advanced Masterclass

Focused on form correction and tournament preparation for experienced archers with personalised coaching feedback.
