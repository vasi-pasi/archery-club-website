Welcome to Al Sah-him Archery Club

A welcoming and inclusive archery club in central London.

Who We Are

The Al Sah-him Archery Club is a long-established society with roots back to 1013 CE and its current home in central London. We are a welcoming and inclusive archery club that combines centuries of tradition with modern facilities and coaching.

Whether you're picking up a bow for the first time or competing at an elite level, you'll find your place here.

Indoor & Outdoor Ranges

Weather-sheltered 18-metre indoor lanes and flexible outdoor distances for all skill levels.

Open to Everyone

Beginner induction courses, junior academies, and coaching for elite tournament archers.

Active Calendar

Seasonal leagues, social nights, and regional target competitions throughout the year.
